
A=control group
B=experiment group

A/B Testing — Two-Proportion Z-Test
├── Define Hypotheses
│   ├── H₀: pA = pB
│   └── H₁: pA ≠ pB
│
├── Calculate Conversion Rates
│   ├── pA = xA / NA
│   └── pB = xB / NB
│
├── Calculate Pooled Proportion
│   └── p̂ = (xA + xB) / (NA + NB)
│
├── Calculate Standard Error
│   └── SE = √[p̂(1 − p̂)(1/NA + 1/NB)]
│
├── Calculate Z-Score
│   └── z = (pB − pA) / SE
│
├── Calculate P-Value
│   └── p-value = 2 × P(Z > |z|)
│
├── Compare P-Value
│   ├── p-value < α → Reject H₀
│   └── p-value ≥ α → Fail to Reject H₀
│
└── Conclusion
    ├── Reject H₀ → Significant difference
    └── Fail to Reject H₀ → No significant difference
